Design choice

A1The filter lists every technique possible within this session, not categories borrowed from the wider library. The chips shown, plus "+4", are the complete set for this transcript.
A2Unlabelled tutor turns stay visible. Roughly half the turns in this transcript carry no move; hiding them would imply the model sees everything.
A3Hovering a highlighted clue lights up the matching phrase in the transcript.
A4Coding the coders never agreed on is labelled "Uncertain" here, rather than the raw disagreement value or a hidden confidence score.
A5Feedback is optional. "Yes" confirms right where you are; "Not quite" or "I'd label it differently" open a short follow-up page to capture the correction, shown in screen 02.
A6Accessibility stays a single circle in the corner, with its options always visible next to it — not buried in a settings menu.
A7Rates the exemplar itself — would you use this clip with your class — kept separate from rating the AI's explanation above it.
